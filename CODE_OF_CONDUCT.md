# Code of conduct

Hopline is a friendly project. Everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else
the project lives) is expected to:

- be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers;
- assume good intent, and discuss ideas, not people;
- give and accept constructive feedback gracefully;
- keep conversations on topic and free of harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.

Not acceptable: harassment of any kind, sexualised language or imagery, personal attacks, trolling, publishing
someone's private information, or anything you wouldn't say to a colleague face to face.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, email the maintainer at **vishwajitmall50@gmail.com**. Reports
are handled privately. The maintainer may remove comments, close issues, or block participants who don't follow
this code.
